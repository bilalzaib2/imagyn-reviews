# Imagyn Reviews V2 Roadmap

> Features intentionally postponed until after the successful launch of Imagyn Reviews on the Shopify App Store.

## Philosophy

V1 focuses on stability, performance, merchant trust, and Shopify approval.

V2 focuses on AI, branding, automation, and innovation.

## Planned Features

- Brand Studio™
- AI Brand Match™
- Live Widget Preview
- AI Email Builder
- Widget Presets
- AI Product Insights
- Review DNA™

No V2 feature should delay App Store launch.
